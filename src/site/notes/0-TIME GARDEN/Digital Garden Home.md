# ✧ Bretts Digital Garden

### 👋 About Me
- Hello, I’m **Brett Holt**, based in **Burgess Hill, West Sussex (UK)**.  
- This site is part of my journey into using **Obsidian** for daily journaling, note-taking, and cultivating a personal digital garden — a place to think, learn, and connect ideas over time.
- Outside of note-making, I’m a keen **tabletop gamer**, with a particular love for **Dungeons & Dragons** and **Blood on the Clocktower**. I enjoy the creativity, storytelling, and social problem-solving that come from shared games around a table.

- Professionally, I’m also a **Table Tennis Sports Coach**, where I focus on helping players develop their skills, confidence, and enjoyment of the sport.

If you’d like to get in touch, you’re very welcome to email me at:  
📧 **brettholtuk@gmail.com**

Thanks for stopping by — feel free to explore.
